Smart Attendance Platform

Develop a modern attendance tracking system for campus events and activities using cutting-edge edge-computing and mobile integration.

description Problem Statement

Current manual attendance systems at Father Agnel CRCE are prone to errors, time-consuming, and difficult to manage for high-volume events such as technical fests, guest lectures, and departmental workshops. We need a unified platform that eliminates the "pen-and-paper" bottleneck while ensuring data integrity and real-time reporting.

Background

The campus currently utilizes disparate Google Sheets and physical registers, making it impossible to track cross-departmental participation trends.

Why it matters

Efficient tracking enables automated certificate generation, better resource allocation, and verifiable student participation records for accreditation.

Expected campus impact

A 90% reduction in entry-time latency for large events and a centralized dashboard for faculty to monitor student engagement metrics across four years of undergraduate study.
